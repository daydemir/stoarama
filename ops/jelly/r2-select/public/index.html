What the options mean

Nothing: R2 holds no footage for this stream; the NAS copy is the only one.
Kept period, stitched only: the stitched hour files for the 28 kept days. Smallest useful option. The V85 re-stitch outputs (about 1.8 TB so far) aren't counted per stream yet and will be added once stitching finishes.
Kept period, raw + stitched: the original 1-minute clips plus stitched hours for the 28 kept days, enough to re-stitch later.
Everything: every day ever recorded, raw and stitched. Days outside the kept period would then exist only in R2.

"Best 14" = the 14 local days with the most recorded seconds, excluding clips known to be bad. Sizes come from the database catalog on Nov 7.
