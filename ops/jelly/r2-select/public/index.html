How coverage and keep options work

Coverage is the union of recorded clip seconds in each stream-local day ÷ 86,400, including gap days. Green is above 90%, yellow is 70–90%, red is below 70%. An empty outlined day has 0%. Daylight-saving days still use 86,400 seconds. Tap a day for its exact coverage.

Nothing: R2 holds no footage; NAS is the only copy. Kept period, stitched: stitched hours in the pinned 28 days. Kept period, raw + stitched: also keeps the original clips for re-stitching. Everything: all days, including footage outside the NAS window.

The pinned best 14 maximizes recorded seconds excluding known bad clips; it is not recalculated here. Storage figures are catalog estimates. V85 re-stitch outputs outside that catalog are not included.
